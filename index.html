Layers and export

PNGs are black on transparent, 20 px/mm (1260 x 1760). SVGs contain one solid shape per layer: a white base plate, and black for every other layer. Overlapping lines, text and art are merged into a single outline (0.05 mm grid), so each layer imports into the slicer as one object at exact 63 x 88 mm scale. Stack layers bottom to top as listed. The set symbol is an empty outline for you to place your own icon.
